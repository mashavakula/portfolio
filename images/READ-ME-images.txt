PUT YOUR IMAGES IN THIS FOLDER.

Every picture on the site lives here. To show an image on a page, the page
refers to it by filename, e.g.  <img src="../images/streets-zoom-out-diagram-1.png">.

Tips:
- Use simple filenames: lowercase, no spaces (use-dashes-like-this.png).
- Keep files reasonably small (under ~1 MB each) so pages load fast.
- Supported formats: .png .jpg .jpeg .gif .webp .svg
- The homepage uses:  images/mtl-stalker-map.png
- The streets example uses: images/streets-zoom-out-diagram-1.png and -2.png
  Add those files (or change the filenames inside the pages to match yours).
